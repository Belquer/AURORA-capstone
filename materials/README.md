# Materials

**Owner:** MEM · **Works with:** production, financials

**Goal:** choose the disc blank, inks and coatings that print sharp cells, stay flat,
survive handling, and keep the disc under $5 (both sides).

## Decided

- Blank thickness starts at 1.0 mm; test 0.75 to 1.5 mm.
- No white ink layer if the blank is already white.
- Avoid PLA (softens around 55 °C) and untreated polypropylene (ink adhesion).

## Candidates

| Blank | Why | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White recycled PET (rPET), 1.0 mm | Default: cheap, flat, #1 recyclable | Softens around 70 °C |
| White ABS, 1.2–1.5 mm, smooth gloss | Best UV-ink adhesion, holds shape to ~90–100 °C, lighter | Recycling code #7; yellows in sunlight; no acetone |
| Aluminium, 0.5 mm | Premium, endlessly recyclable | Needs primer and white base |
| Upcycled vinyl LP | Reuses scratched records; right size, weight and hole | Grooves under the cells; paper labels |
| Coated card | Promo singles, zines | Warps with humidity |
